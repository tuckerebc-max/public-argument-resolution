# Public Argument Resolution handoffs

## From Argumentation — `HOF-ARG-PAR-001`

Require claim map, evidence ledger, strongest alternative, uncertainty record, affected parties, authority, decision rule, source IDs, evidence IDs, claim IDs, provenance IDs, and learner decisions. If authority or criteria are missing, stop before resolution.

## To Human Review — `HOF-PAR-REVIEW-001`

Pass decision or unresolved record, dissent record, review trigger, authority, provenance, source/evidence/uncertainty IDs, learner decisions, and AI-use log. The human reviewer owns any consequential decision.

## Return to Argumentation or QUEST

Route missing warrant, weak alternative, evidence conflict, causal gap, or missing research support to the appropriate upstream skill without discarding the public issue record.
