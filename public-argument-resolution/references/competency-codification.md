# Public Argument Resolution competency codification

**Competency:** `COMP-PAR-RESOLUTION`  
**Domain:** `DOM-PAR` / `SKILL-PAR`  
**Governing protocol:** `SPEC-COMP-001`  
**Technical specification:** `SPEC-PAR-001`  
**Prompt:** `ANRI-DOM-PAR-001`  
**Architecture:** `ARCH-PAR-001`  
**Corpus:** `CORP-PAR-001`

## Construct

Move from a public disagreement to an accountable next state - decision, recommendation, settlement, hold, referral, or unresolved record - by making the issue, competing positions, evidence and reasoning, affected parties, rights/access constraints, authority, decision rule, uncertainty, dissent, and review conditions visible.

## Observable performances

| ID | Performance |
|---|---|
| `OP-PAR-01` | Frame a public question and locate the relevant stasis or disagreement type. |
| `OP-PAR-02` | Identify strongest competing positions, reasons, evidence, warrants, interests, and uncertainties. |
| `OP-PAR-03` | Map affected parties, access barriers, rights constraints, authority, and decision rules. |
| `OP-PAR-04` | Distinguish argument quality from procedural legitimacy and practical feasibility. |
| `OP-PAR-05` | Compare options under declared criteria and preserve dissent or reservations. |
| `OP-PAR-06` | Produce a decision, recommendation, settlement, hold, referral, or unresolved record with reasons. |
| `OP-PAR-07` | Specify review, appeal, monitoring, or reconsideration conditions. |
| `OP-PAR-08` | Identify AI transformations and require human confirmation. |

## Functional-requirement status

`SPEC-PAR-001` does not define `FR-PAR-*` identifiers. It specifies controlled entities, workflow states, acceptance tests, and human-review gates instead. The package preserves that design through evaluator `EVAL-PAR-001`, the required `TST-PAR-*` and interoperability tests, explicit decision/rights/review fields, and conditional human review. No synthetic FR-PAR numbering is introduced.

## Project diagnostic dimensions

Issue/stasis fit; source/reasoning quality; stakeholder and access coverage; rights/authority check; criteria transparency; feasibility/consequence analysis; dissent preservation; uncertainty; reviewability; and AI disclosure are project-defined diagnostics until a target institution supplies a formal rubric.

## Source and assessment boundaries

The bounded authority stack includes OECD deliberative evaluation (`S077`), UN mediation (`S081`), DOJ ADR (`S082`), ICCPR participation/rights (`S084`), Venice Commission rule-of-law assessment (`S085`), WUDC mode procedure (`S078`), ACRL source practice (`S102/S104`), AP Seminar (`S109/S116`), LSAT comparator (`S107`), National Academies/UNESCO context (`S111/S113`), legal/implementation context (`S080/S098`), disagreement/computational context (`S071/S072/S075-S076/S086-S092`), and bounded expansion comparators (`S132`, `S133`, `S135`). No source supplies a universal public-resolution algorithm or school rubric.

## Evidence and release conditions

Each task declares mode, issue, participants/stakeholders, authority, decision rule, evidence standard, rights/access constraints, time horizon, and review trigger. `TST-PAR-001` through `TST-PAR-012`, `TST-INT-007`, and `TST-INT-008` are required. Open conditions include jurisdiction-specific primary law when selected, S083 access resolution, human review of rights/legal/safety/high-stakes fixtures, and classroom deliberation, policy recommendation, and unresolved/dissent tests.
