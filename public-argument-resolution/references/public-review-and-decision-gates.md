# Public review and decision gates

## Required gates

1. Issue and stasis are bounded.
2. Authority and jurisdiction are named.
3. Affected parties, access needs, rights, and conflict-of-interest conditions are visible.
4. Competing positions are reconstructed in their strongest recognizable form.
5. Evidence has locators, source role, method or basis, currency, limitations, rights status, and direct-reading status.
6. Criteria and decision rule are explicit, including what the rule cannot decide.
7. Dissent, uncertainty, missing information, implementation risks, and revision triggers are recorded.
8. Human authority confirms any recommendation, decision, settlement, or adjudication.

Do not treat a decision outcome as proof that one position was factually true. Do not force consensus in mediation or deliberation. Route legal, civic, policy, health, disciplinary, or other high-consequence decisions to accountable human review.
